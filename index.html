Beautifully Bristolian

From Corporate Bristol to Clifton's Favorite Family Bistro

Pigeons was founded in the summer of 2022 by Edward Pigeon. Leaving the corporate world of KPMG behind, Ed pursued his true dream: building an independent foodie sanctuary celebrating wholesome, delicious British produce in the heart of Clifton.

Nothing is rushed, nothing is sourced from afar. Whether it’s sourdough baked around the corner, free-range sausages from Wiltshire, or oak-cured salmon from the Severn, our food represents the pinnacle of modern British cafe culture.
